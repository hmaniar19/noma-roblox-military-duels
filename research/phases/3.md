# Phase 3: Maps and drop-ins

Goal: all three maps and Call of Duty-style insertions. Earlier phases must keep working.

In scope (brief sections 2.1, 3):
1. **Maps:** Desert Outpost and Urban Training Town are added next to the Shooting Range. All three are medium-big and built from code.
   - Each has hills and dips, two-story buildings with windows, and one tall central tower.
   - Out-of-bounds volumes seal every gap, and there are no spots inside geometry where a player can hide.
2. Each match picks one map.
3. **Drop-ins:** each round randomly picks one of four:
   - helicopter fast-rope
   - army truck
   - parachute jump
   - armored car

   The loadout pick happens during the ride. The ride lasts about 10 seconds and is scripted on the server, and players can't act during it. The spawn shield starts when you land.
4. Bots can navigate all three maps.

Not yet: cutscenes, lobby, economy.
