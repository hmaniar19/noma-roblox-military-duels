# Phase 5: Economy and progression

Goal: saved coins, XP, levels and unlocks. Earlier phases must keep working.

In scope (brief sections 4, 6):
1. **Saving:** coins, XP, level, the "beginnersluck" flag, owned items, the starter pick, and HUD colors are saved per account with DataStoreService.
   - Only the server writes them.
   - Save on leave and on server close, and keep working if the DataStore isn't available (for example in Studio without API access).
2. **Payouts:**
   - The match winner gets 120 coins if the final shot was a headshot, or 80 if it wasn't.
   - The loser gets 10 for each round they won.
   - New players get 350 coins once per account with the message "beginnersluck".
3. **XP** comes from wins, eliminations and finishing matches. The curve is tuned so Level 100 takes about 2–3 weeks of normal play.
4. **Unlocks:**
   - Level 50: Ranked mode.
   - Level 100: Double Trouble (twin dark-red glowing swords, red trail, a *shing* on draw).
   - Level 200: the "Eddie's Honor" title and a gold salute emote.
   - Level 300: A Mother's Wrath (dark-blue glowing rifle with a blue-lightning elimination cutscene).
   - Unlockables are cosmetic only: same stats as the weapon they replace.
5. **Shop:** coins buy weapon skins and anime finishers. All emotes are free.
6. **Starter pick:** on first join, choose a starter weapon and its base color.
7. **Leaderboard** in Camp Moore.

Hard rule: no real-money purchases.
