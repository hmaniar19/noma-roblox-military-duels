# Phase 4: Spectacle and Camp More Maniar

Goal: the cutscenes, the lobby and the tribute. Earlier phases must keep working.

In scope (brief sections 2.3, 2.4, 8):
1. **Bullet cam:** if the round-winning shot was a headshot, play a slow-motion shot of the bullet flying, then "HEADSHOT — CRITICAL HIT".
2. **Anime finisher:** after each round win, show a freeze frame, dramatic zoom, speed lines and the winner's name card, with the winner standing over the defeated player.
   - Blood is anime ink only: red slash marks and ink splatter.
   - It must stay within Roblox's Mild maturity label.
3. **Draws:** both teams walk to the middle and do a handshake cutscene.
4. Cutscenes run on the client, but the server decides when they start. The next round can't start until every client has finished or a timeout passes.
5. **Camp More Maniar lobby:**
   - A military-base courtyard.
   - A free Shooting Range where you can try any weapon.
   - A spot for the shop (phase 5) and a leaderboard.
   - In the middle, a statue of Joanna and Eddie Moore side by side, holding hands, with their names underneath.
6. A loading screen that reads "Military Duels — dedicated to Joanna & Eddie Moore."
7. A HUD color picker on first join (default Navy & Gold), which can be changed later in Settings. Saving it comes in phase 5.
