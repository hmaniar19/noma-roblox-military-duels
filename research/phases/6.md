# Phase 6: Anti-cheat enforcement and matchmaking

Goal: catching cheaters, Bot Bootcamp, reports, and real matchmaking. Earlier phases must keep working.

In scope (brief section 7):
1. **Cheat flags:** phase 1 already collects them. Now flags build up into a "flagged" status, and they must not produce false positives from normal lag. Use tolerances, decay and thresholds.
2. **Bot Bootcamp:**
   - Flagged players are matched only against server bots that play perfectly.
   - They get out by finishing 3 clean matches in a row with zero flags.
   - Bootcamp status is saved per account.
3. **Report button:** saves a server-side record of the last round (positions and shots over time) that can be reviewed.
4. **Matchmaking:**
   - A 1v1 and a 2v2 queue from Camp More Maniar.
   - Bots fill the queue after a wait.
   - Leaving mid-match counts as a loss and is saved.
5. **Exploit review:** go back over every RemoteEvent and RemoteFunction. Rate-limit them, type-check them, and never trust values from the client.
