# Phase 2: Arsenal and 2v2

Goal: the full loadout, Sudden Death, and 2v2. Phase 1 must keep working.

In scope (brief sections 1, 4):
1. **Loadout = 1 primary + 1 sidearm + 1 melee + 1 gadget**, re-picked every round on a pre-round screen.
2. **Primaries:**
   - Assault rifle.
   - SMG: fast, strong up close, weak at range.
   - Shotgun: one-shot up close, useless at range.
   - Sniper (owner decision 2026-11-21: a normal primary for everyone).
   - Plus a sidearm and a combat knife.
   - All stats come from one shared weapon config and are enforced on the server.
3. **Gadgets:**
   - Smoke grenade: blocks line of sight for players and bots.
   - Frag grenade: server-side damage with falloff, blocked by walls.
   - Med kit: +50 HP over a 3-second channel during which you can't shoot. It is interrupted by taking damage.
4. **Sudden Death at 4–4:**
   - 2v2: one player per team gets the sniper and the teammate gets the combat knife. The team chooses who before the round.
   - 1v1: each player picks sniper or knife.
   - Sudden Death forces one sniper and one knife per team. Owner decision (2026-11-21): the sniper is also a normal primary for everyone outside Sudden Death.
5. **2v2 mode:** bots fill any empty slots on either team. A team wins the round when all opponents are eliminated.
6. Mode select: 1v1 or 2v2, with a simple menu for now.

Not yet: other maps, drop-ins, cutscenes, lobby, economy.
